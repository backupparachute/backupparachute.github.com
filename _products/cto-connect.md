## CTO level thinking without hiring one

CTO Connect gives founders, first time engineering leads, and technical decision makers access to a 27
year operator who has seen the failure modes and knows how to avoid them.

Most vendors sell generic advice. CTO Connect gives real answers for _your_ situation. The rewrite
debate. The hire versus contract call. The architecture decision that will bind you for three years.
The process issue that is actually a personnel issue.

## Who it is for

- Non technical founders making technical bets
- First time engineering leads running a small team
- Agency and freelance owners negotiating scope
- Small dev teams (two to five people) making compounding decisions

## Pricing

**$99 a month**, thirty day free trial, cancel anytime. Deep Dive sessions are $600 each. Fractional
engagements start at $2,000 a month.

## Why BPS built it

Kyle has seen $50M rewrites that never shipped, and one person teams that ship faster than fifty person
ones. CTO Connect is how he shares that pattern recognition with the founders who need it most. Priced
so it is accessible. Structured so it actually helps.
