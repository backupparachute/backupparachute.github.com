## Built for the auto transport industry

VehicHaul is the transportation management system (TMS) and electronic proof of delivery (ePOD) mobile app
built for moving finished vehicles. Drivers capture signatures, photos, and damage exceptions on
iOS or Android. Dispatchers manage loads, invoicing, and EDI partners from a single dashboard.

## Why BPS built it

Auto transport has unique requirements. VINs, damage maps, lot locations, railhead coordination. Generic
TMS platforms never handle all of them well. VehicHaul was designed from the ground up for this workflow.
It has been running in production for years, trusted by operators who depend on getting a truck full of cars
to its destination without surprises.
