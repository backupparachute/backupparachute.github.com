## Partnerships, aligned on outcomes

Meddly (also known as Acuerdo) is an AI powered partnership engine for lifestyle and consumer brands.
It helps growth and partnership teams find collaborators that actually move sales. Not the ones that just
look good in a press release.

## Why BPS built it

Most partnership tooling optimizes for reach or engagement. That is a lagging indicator at best and a
vanity metric at worst. Meddly was built on a different premise. The best brand partnerships are the ones
that move revenue. Alignment of audience, positioning, and business model is what makes them work.

## Who it is for

Lifestyle and consumer brands running partnership programs who want to make the shift from "we did a
collab" to "we did a collab that moved the number."
