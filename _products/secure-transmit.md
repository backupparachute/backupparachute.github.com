## Integration without the integration team

Secure Transmit lets small teams connect systems that were never meant to talk to each other.
ERP to WMS, trading partner to warehouse, spreadsheet to API. No specialized integrator required.
No glue code to write from scratch.

## Why BPS built it

Every business eventually needs to move data between systems. Most SMBs cannot justify the six figure
integration platforms built for enterprises. Secure Transmit fills the gap with the right feature set
and the right price.
