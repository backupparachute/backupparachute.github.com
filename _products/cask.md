## A personal service for whiskey collectors

Cask helps whiskey enthusiasts manage their collections, remember what they have tried, and find what to try next.
It is lightweight, easy to use, and built by someone who actually enjoys a good pour.

## Why BPS built it

Most spirits tracking apps are bloated or tied to a single retailer. Cask is different. A clean, personal
notebook for your whiskey journey, with community reviews when you want a second opinion.
