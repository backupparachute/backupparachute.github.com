## Metrics that actually matter

DevMetrics is a set of free browser based calculators and essays for engineering leaders who want to
improve their teams. It focuses on the DORA metrics that predict real outcomes. Deployment frequency,
lead time for changes, change failure rate, and PR size.

## The argument

Story points measure output. Output is not outcomes. The fastest way to fix a slow team is to make them
ship more often. Everything else follows.

## Why BPS built it

After running production SaaS for over a decade, we have a strong opinion about what makes a dev team
effective. DevMetrics is where we publish that opinion, with tools to let other teams measure themselves
against it.
