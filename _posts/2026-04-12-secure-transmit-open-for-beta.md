If you have ever wired together two business systems by forwarding files through email, you already know the problem Secure Transmit solves.

B2B integrations are a solved problem at the top of the market. If you have a team of engineers and a budget with six figures on it, you can buy your way into a data pipeline that works. Everyone below that line is still doing it with duct tape. Manual file uploads. Excel macros. Somebody on your ops team running the same SFTP drop every morning.

That is the gap we are filling.

## What Secure Transmit is

A no code platform for moving data between your systems and your partners. You build pipelines visually. You plug in EDI, SFTP, API, or plain file based exchange. The platform handles the schedule, the retries, the alerting, and the audit trail.

## Who it is for

Small and mid sized businesses with real data workflows who cannot justify an enterprise integration platform. Trading partners pushing EDI. Operations teams chasing down recurring file transfers. Anyone with a Rube Goldberg machine built out of shell scripts and hope.

## Current state

We are in closed beta. Pipelines are running in production for early customers. Onboarding is hands on while we tune the product. If you want in, [request access](/contact/) and tell us what you are trying to connect. We read every request.
